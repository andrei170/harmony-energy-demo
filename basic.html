Why us

Designed in-house. Nothing handed to a sales outfit.

A lot of what gets sold in this market is sold by one company and fitted by another. That is how people end up with an array on a roof that should not have carried one.

How we work

  • NABCEP certified installers, in-house
  • Design and installation by the same people
  • One point of contact from survey to commissioning
  • We service what we install, and systems we did not
  • Prior experience taking commercial customers through funding

What we will not do

  • Promise a grant, a percentage or a date
  • Quote a system before seeing the building
  • Put panels on a roof that needs replacing first
  • Hand you to a subcontractor after the sale

If the roof is near the end of its life, we will say so

It is the most expensive mistake in this industry and it is usually made by somebody who will not be around in five years when it matters. We would rather lose the job than be the reason you pay to take an array off again.
