The grant, in plain terms.

Not a discount, not financing. A federal programme that funds part of a renewable energy project for businesses that qualify — and qualifying is narrower and more specific than most people assume.

What business owners ask first.

What actually is the REAP grant?

A USDA programme supporting renewable energy projects for eligible rural small businesses and agricultural producers. Eligibility depends on location, business type and size, and applications run to published windows rather than all year.

How do I know if we are eligible?

Ask us and we will tell you early and for free. The main factors are where the business sits, what it does and its size. We would rather tell you no on the first call than after a survey.

Do you do the grant application for us?

We are installers, not grant consultants. We produce the technical documentation the application needs and keep you to the deadline. We do not decide who receives funding.

What if we do not qualify?

Then we look at whether the project stands up on its own without it. Sometimes it does. If it does not, we will say so.

What does NABCEP certification actually mean?

It is the recognised North American certification for solar PV installers. It separates firms that install from firms that sell and subcontract.

Do you work outside New Hampshire?

Yes — New Hampshire, Maine and Massachusetts.
